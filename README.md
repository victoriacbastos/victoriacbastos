# Your portfolio site — quick start

This folder is your website:

- `index.html` — the site itself. Double-click it to open in your browser right now.
- `headshot.jpg` — your photo.
- `albums/` — the four cover thumbnails for the "Replay" section at the bottom.

Everything is filled in — name, Tal Research Group and LinkedIn and ORCID links, bio,
research interests, education (with your CAPES/Yale fellowship and both advisors), three
publications linked to their journal pages, honors, and the Replay section. Open `index.html`
in a text editor (TextEdit, VS Code, etc.) if you want to tweak wording, add more
publications, or update anything later — just don't touch anything inside `<...>` angle
brackets, that's the site's structure.
