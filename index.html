About

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Tal Research Group at MIT.

My research focuses on immunopathogenesis, biomarkers, and clinical assessments of infection-associated chronic illnesses.

Before MIT, I worked as an emergency physician at Clínica São Vicente in Rio de Janeiro, where I also helped develop an AI-assisted patient charting tool. I received my MD and PhD in Immunology and Inflammation from UFRJ, Brazil, under the supervision of Marcelo Bozza, with a CAPES PrInt fellowship at Yale University under Akiko Iwasaki. During the COVID-19 pandemic, I worked as a volunteer medical student at the UFRJ COVID-19 testing center under Terezinha Castiñeiras.

I'm dedicated to helping understand why some people recover from infection and others don't, and how we can help change this trajectory.

Research Interests

Immunology, infectious diseases, Long COVID, long Lyme, ME/CFS, infection-associated chronic illnesses, capillaroscopy, and artificial intelligence.
